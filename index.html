01 About

I turn ambiguous business problems into structured, defensible analytics.

I’m an M.S. candidate in Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence at Johns Hopkins Carey Business School, with a B.A. in Economics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My background sits where economics, data, and decision-making meet — and I’m especially drawn to the parts most people skip: data quality, KPI definitions, and making analysis traceable.

Focused on data quality, business intelligence, product analytics, technical business analysis, and AI-assisted analytical workflows.

  1. 01

    Start from the decision

    Before I touch the data, I ask what we’re actually deciding and what “good” looks like. Analysis without a decision behind it is just decoration.

  2. 02

    Make the logic auditable

    Metric definitions, assumptions, and validation checks written down — so a conclusion can be questioned, traced, and trusted, not just believed.

  3. 03

    Build for reuse

    A clear summary, the numbers behind it, and an output someone else can pick up without re-explaining. If the team can’t use it, it isn’t done.
