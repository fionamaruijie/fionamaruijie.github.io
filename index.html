QUODD Corporate Action Adjustment Integrity Framework

Analytics Consulting Project · Project Coordinator / Integration Lead · Top-5 finalist

Financial data quality requires traceable anomaly classification and defensible prioritization.

A client-ready issue prioritization framework, “The Adjustment Filter” (filter → diagnose → prioritize), run over 18,923 securities. It produced 4,141 ranked review cases — 2,159 pure-timing, 1,965 mixed, and 17 pure-value issues — narrowed attention to 1,078 high-priority securities (5.7%), and cleared 14,782 (78.1%) for deprioritization.

My part: I integrated three teammates’ anomaly-detection modules (timing errors, factor mismatches, missing reactions), reviewed four teammates’ outputs, drove the timeline and client communication, and opened the final client presentation with the team’s recommendation.

Fuel Inventory Optimization Analysis

Python Analytics Project · Data Engineering & EDA · Three-person team · Sep–Oct 2025

An inventory policy is only as reliable as the merged data underneath it.

My part was data engineering and EDA: a pandas pipeline that read, cleaned, and merged 5 datasets (954 days, 8 stations, 23 tanks, 2,869 purchases), with summary-statistics and data-quality tables — including removing 3.2% duplicate records.

Team finding: 65.4% of orders missed volume discounts. A reorder-point policy (refill to capacity at 15,000 L) would secure the discount on every order while keeping 17.7 days of safety stock.
